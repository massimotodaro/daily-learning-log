## DL-2026-09-05-01 — Revoking authority is immediate; work in flight is reassigned, never destroyed

**Areas:** Security and privacy; Software engineering

**Tags:** `authorization`, `revocation`, `state-models`

### What I learned

Removing someone's authority raises three separate questions: what they can do from now on, what happens to work they had started, and whether their past decisions still stand. Answering only the first leaves orphaned work.

### What I did

I recorded a general principle for every role: revocation is immediate and total for the actor, work in flight moves to a successor, and past valid decisions are never retroactively voided.

### Evidence or example

The principle reuses one fail-closed authority check everywhere, with no special cases, which was also the simplest thing to build.

### Why it matters

Without the second and third answers, removing a teacher could silently lose half-marked work or invalidate grades that were correct when given.

### Open questions

- Who is the default successor when no one has been named?

### Next step

When designing any permission, write the revocation behaviour for in-flight work at the same time.

## DL-2026-09-05-02 — Separate the registry from the content, and inventory every sample

**Areas:** Software engineering; Tools and workflow

**Tags:** `registries`, `sample-data`, `maintainability`

### What I learned

Build the mechanism, not the content. Schema, validation, loading and versioning ship as code; the content that fills them is data added later without a code change.

### What I did

I adopted this as a standing rule for every content-bearing feature: sample content is allowed so the mechanism can be proved early, but it must be unmistakably marked as sample and listed in one inventory.

### Evidence or example

The inventory turns 'replace the placeholders' into a tracked task. An unlisted sample is how a fixture ends up shipped as if it were real.

### Why it matters

It lets engineering move before real content exists, without ever confusing a demonstration with a product.

### Open questions

- Should a build check fail if any sample content is reachable in production?

### Next step

Keep the sample inventory next to the registry it fills, and review it before each release.
