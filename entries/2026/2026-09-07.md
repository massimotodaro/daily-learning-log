## DL-2026-09-07-01 — Model the atomic unit first: the answer is born with the question

**Areas:** Product and design; Education and domain knowledge

**Tags:** `domain-modelling`, `assessment`, `content-design`

### What I learned

Lessons and assignments are combinations of smaller assets. Getting the smallest unit right removes problems higher up: if a question's correct answer, or its marking criteria, are created at the same moment as the question, there is no later step where an answer key must be found.

### What I did

A blocking design question about where marking answers come from dissolved once the unit was defined: multiple-choice and fixed-answer questions store their answer at creation; open questions store their marking criteria instead.

### Evidence or example

The decision replaced a proposed separate answer-sourcing mechanism with a rule at the point of creation.

### Why it matters

Many 'missing source' problems are really a unit defined too late. Moving a fact to the moment it is naturally known is often simpler than building a pipeline to recover it.

### Open questions

- Which other derived facts could be captured at creation instead of reconstructed?

### Next step

When a design needs a new pipeline to recover information, first ask whether it could have been captured earlier.
