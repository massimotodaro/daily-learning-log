## DL-2026-09-18-01 — One app, not two

**Areas:** Product and design; Tools and workflow

**Tags:** `focus`, `design-authority`, `process`

### What I learned

A second design surface that sits beside the real application drifts, and work gets proposed against the wrong one. Deleting it was cheaper than coordinating it.

### What I did

The second application and its references were deleted. The rule became: open the one running app before any design discussion or proposal, and never build mockups unless explicitly asked.

### Evidence or example

Time spent reviewing proposals made against the wrong surface was the cost that triggered the decision.

### Why it matters

For a small team, one source of truth for the interface is worth more than the flexibility of a sandbox.

### Open questions

- When is a throwaway prototype still justified?

### Next step

Before designing, confirm which running application the work belongs to.
