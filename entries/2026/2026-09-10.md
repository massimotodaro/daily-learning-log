## DL-2026-09-10-01 — Assess a model's real behaviour before the vendor's written answers arrive

**Areas:** AI and agent systems; Business and leadership

**Tags:** `evaluation`, `vendor-assessment`, `sequencing`

### What I learned

Contractual and legal answers from a model provider gate real users, but they do not have to gate a synthetic evaluation. Waiting for paperwork before measuring behaviour wastes the time when the most can be learned.

### What I did

I recorded that the assessment of the current safety and cognitive-offloading runtimes would run against the live model route as soon as it was configured, using synthetic cases only, with results marked provisional if the route changes.

### Evidence or example

The route was to be admitted as configuration behind a single egress gateway, never hard-coded, so switching providers stays a configuration change.

### Why it matters

Separating 'safe to test' from 'safe to launch' lets technical evidence and legal evidence progress in parallel.

### Open questions

- What must be re-run if the admitted route changes after the assessment?

### Next step

Tag every evaluation result with the exact route it ran against.
