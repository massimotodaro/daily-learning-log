## DL-2026-09-17-01 — Jurisdiction is a rule pack, not a superset

**Areas:** Business and leadership; Software engineering

**Tags:** `compliance`, `internationalisation`, `configuration`

### What I learned

No country's rules are a strict subset of another's: one is heaviest on safeguarding, another on lawful basis or AI regulation. Treating the home country as the strictest case, and assuming the rest follow, is unsafe.

### What I did

I recorded an architecture where each jurisdiction is a versioned data pack (lawful bases per sector, age bands, school year, time zone, retention and more) read through one resolver. A literal country code had spread through dozens of files and several database constraints; a check now fails on such literals.

### Evidence or example

Going to a new country becomes writing a pack, not a rewrite. Behaviour a pack needs but the code lacks is honestly flagged as new code.

### Why it matters

Hard-coded compliance values are the most likely to change and the most expensive to find later.

### Open questions

- How should a pack be reviewed and signed off before it ships?

### Next step

Treat any compliance literal in code as a defect and route it through the resolver.

## DL-2026-09-17-02 — The safety floor is not a product tier

**Areas:** AI and agent systems; Education and domain knowledge

**Tags:** `safety`, `pricing`, `guardrails`

### What I learned

Tiers and prices may change what a product can do, never how well it protects children. Content filtering, safeguarding detection, alerts, usage limits and crisis response must be identical in every tier.

### What I did

I recorded the rule and asked for it to be proved by tests across every entitlement state, because the principle already existed in writing but no test demonstrated it.

### Evidence or example

A check the same day found that the safety path read no commercial value; the gap was the missing proof, not the behaviour.

### Why it matters

Commercial pressure tends to arrive later. A tested floor stops a future pricing change from quietly weakening protection.

### Open questions

- What is the smallest test that would fail if a tier ever touched the safety path?

### Next step

Add structural tests that the safety path cannot import commercial code.
